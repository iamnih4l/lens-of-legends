North Kerala — Theyyam in the Sacred Grove

Chapter I: Firelight of the Kavu (Playable Vertical Slice)

1. VERIFIED CULTURAL TRADITION (Authentic Historical & Cultural Context)
Theyyam (also known as Kaliyattam) is a centuries-old living ritual worship tradition practiced across sacred groves (Kavus) and village shrines in North Malabar, Kerala (primarily Kannur and Kasaragod districts) between December and May. Following the vocal Thottam invocation and intricate Mukhathezhuthu facial painting, the performer is revered as a living manifestation of the deity or ancestral hero. (Source: Wikipedia — Theyyam)

2. STORYTELLER INTRODUCTION (Fictional Narrative Layer)
You arrive at dusk in a humid village courtyard in North Malabar. Local mentor and archivist Madhavan Nair welcomes you beside the laterite wall of an ancestral Kavu. Bronze oil lamps (Nilavilakku), a 36-lamp Chuttuvilakku shrine frame, and a ceremonial bonfire (Meleri) illuminate the red earth as the rhythmic pulse of the Chenda drum echoes through coconut and areca palms.
